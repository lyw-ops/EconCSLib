# Sequential-Equilibrium Lean Foundation

## Scope

The first implementation targets a discrete `ObservedChanceGame` with a finite
player type and an `ObservedGame.FiniteEFGHypotheses` certificate. The latter
supplies a uniformly bounded, locally finite complete-history unfolding, finite
information-state carriers, representation of every declared decision
information state, and mover coherence. A separate recall certificate supplies
perfect recall.

The carrier of nodes inside information state `information` for player `i` is

```lean
G.observed.DecisionInfoWitness i information
```

and therefore retains a complete history occurrence, the mover and
nonterminal proofs, and the equality identifying the history's information
state. Equal endpoint states reached through different histories remain
different belief points.

## Proposed signatures and invariants

Raw belief weights and normalized beliefs remain separate:

```lean
RawBeliefSystem G :=
  (i : N) → (information : G.observed.InfoState i) →
    G.observed.DecisionInfoWitness i information → ℝ≥0∞

BeliefSystem G :=
  (i : N) → (information : G.observed.InfoState i) →
    PMF (G.observed.DecisionInfoWitness i information)
```

An assessment pairs a behavioral profile with a normalized belief system.
Normalization is structural because each information-state belief is a
`PMF`; the API must also expose the theorem that its `tsum` is one.

For a behavioral profile, the reach weight of a decision occurrence is the
probability assigned to its complete history by the existing
terminal-aware stochastic history executor at fuel equal to the history
length. The Bayes belief at an information state is the normalization of
these reach weights and is defined only from a proof that their total is
nonzero. The finite-EFG certificate proves that the denominator is finite.

A behavioral profile is completely mixed when every abstract action at every
declared information state has positive probability. Kreps--Wilson
consistency is certified by one sequence of completely mixed behavioral
profiles such that:

1. every information state has positive total reach under every perturbation;
2. action probabilities converge pointwise to the assessment's behavioral
   profile; and
3. the corresponding Bayes beliefs converge pointwise to the assessment's
   beliefs.

This is a topology-level `Filter.Tendsto` statement on `ℝ≥0∞`, not a custom
epsilon relation. In particular, Bayes' rule is never used to choose beliefs
directly at a zero-probability information state of the limiting profile.

Sequential rationality is initially parameterized by a local continuation
value evaluator:

```lean
SequentialDecisionEvaluator.value :
  Assessment G → (i : N) → (information : G.observed.InfoState i) →
    PMF (G.observed.InfoAction i information) → ℝ
```

The predicate compares the assessment's action law with every local
behavioral deviation. This interface can already express genuine
belief-dependent off-path rationality, but it is not yet the canonical
expected-continuation-payoff evaluator.

## Proved boundary and remaining bridge

The initial module should prove:

- normalized beliefs have total mass one;
- finite Bayes normalization is well-defined under positive reach;
- the Bayes formula at every decision occurrence;
- projections from consistency to both pointwise convergence statements; and
- evaluator-relative sequential equilibrium implies consistency and
  sequential rationality.

It must not yet claim that this evaluator-relative predicate implies Nash,
weak sequential equilibrium, standard sequential equilibrium, or SPE.
Promotion to a stable equilibrium facade requires a canonical conditional
continuation-utility evaluator, equivalence with the textbook local
optimality definition, and the finite-perfect-recall theorem that the
resulting sequential equilibrium induces a Nash equilibrium.
